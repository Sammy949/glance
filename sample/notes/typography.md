# Typography

Notes on setting long-form text on screen.

## Measure

Sixty to seventy characters per line. Shorter for dense reference material,
longer only when the reader is scanning rather than reading.

## Rhythm

Line height carries the page. Too tight and paragraphs turn into blocks; too
loose and the eye loses the thread between lines.

## Contrast

Body text needs a real gap from its background. Muted is fine for labels, not
for the sentence someone is actually reading.
